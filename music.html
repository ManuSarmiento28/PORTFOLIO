Arrangements and Covers

01

Saturn Orchestral Arrangement

I created an orchestral arrangement of “Saturn” using FL Studio as the main production platform. The entire project was developed through piano recording and direct composition within the software, without using any pre-recorded samples. My goal was to recreate the emotional and cinematic atmosphere of the original piece, exploring the dynamics of strings, brass, and harmonic textures to craft a unique and personal orchestral interpretation.

02

Solas by Jamie Duffy

“Solas” is one of my favorite pieces for its deep emotion and expressive atmosphere. This song challenged me both technically and emotionally, as it required precision, control, and sensitivity to capture its intensity. Learning and performing it was a rewarding experience that strengthened my connection to the piano.

03

Comptine d'un autre été by Yann Tiersen

“Comptine d'un autre été” is a piece I enjoy for its delicate, reflective, and emotional character. Its repetitive patterns create a sense of calm while still requiring control, consistency, and expression throughout the performance. Learning this piece helped me focus on dynamics, timing, and maintaining an emotional connection to the music, making it a meaningful addition to my piano repertoire.

04

Where's My Love

For this cover of “Where’s My Love,” I collaborated with a classmate who performed the vocals while I played guitar. I really enjoy interpreting songs like this because they have a soft, emotional, and intimate feeling that allows me to connect more deeply with the music. Playing guitar for this piece gave me the opportunity to focus on supporting the vocals while still bringing out the emotion of the song. I especially enjoy this type of performance because it feels more personal and expressive, and I like how a simple arrangement can still create a strong emotional atmosphere.
