Saturn Orchestral Arrangement

I created an orchestral arrangement of “Saturn” using FL Studio as the main production platform. The entire project was developed through piano recording and direct composition within the software, without using any pre-recorded samples. My goal was to recreate the emotional and cinematic atmosphere of the original piece, exploring the dynamics of strings, brass, and harmonic textures to craft a unique and personal orchestral interpretation.

02

Solas by Jamie Duffy

“Solas” is one of my favorite pieces for its deep emotion and expressive atmosphere. This song challenged me both technically and emotionally, as it required precision, control, and sensitivity to capture its intensity. Learning and performing it was a rewarding experience that strengthened my connection to the piano.
